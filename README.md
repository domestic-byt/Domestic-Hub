DomesticHub

Connecting Ethiopian businesses, producers, service providers, and customers through one digital platform.

DomesticHub is a digital marketplace project designed to help Ethiopian businesses reach more customers and make it easier for people to discover and purchase products and services.

Vision

Build a trusted digital marketplace that connects local producers, merchants, service providers, and customers across Ethiopia.

Planned Features

- Product Marketplace: Discover and browse products from local businesses.
- Product Categories: Organize products for easy discovery.
- Business Profiles: Help merchants showcase their products and businesses.
- Product Details: Display product descriptions, images, and prices.
- Search: Help customers find products and businesses.
- User Accounts: Support customer and merchant accounts.
- Orders: Provide an organized purchasing experience.
- Mobile-Friendly Design: Make the platform accessible on smartphones and computers.

Technology

The technology stack will be documented as the platform is developed and integrated.

Development Status

DomesticHub is under development. Features listed above represent planned functionality and may not yet be implemented.
